Welcome to RoboPet AI.

Design and Development of an AI-Driven Robotic Pet for Enhanced Companion and Interactive Storytelling

Abstract

RoboPet AI is an innovative AI-driven robotic pet designed to bridge the emotional gap. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence, NLP, and smart sensors, RoboPet AI interacts with users, detects their emotional state, and engages them in interactive storytelling. It serves as a smart, empathetic companion for children and the elderly.
