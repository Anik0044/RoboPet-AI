About RoboPet AI

Bridging the gap between technology and emotional connection.

Abstract

RoboPet AI is an innovative AI-driven robotic pet designed to serve as a smart, empathetic companion. In today's fast-paced digital world, loneliness and lack of interactive engagement are growing concerns. Traditional toys lack the intelligence to provide meaningful companionship or adapt to a user's emotional needs.

By leveraging Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and smart sensors, our robot interacts with users, detects their emotional state, and engages them in interactive storytelling. It serves as a friendly presence for children and the elderly, promoting mental well-being and offering a unique interactive experience.

What Our Robot Does

🗣️

Voice Interaction

Uses AI and NLP to understand voice commands and respond naturally, just like a real pet.

📖

Interactive Storytelling

Engages users by narrating stories and adapting the plot based on the user's choices.

❤️

Smart Companion

Detects touch and movement, providing emotional support and companionship to reduce loneliness.
