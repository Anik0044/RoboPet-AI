ABOUT PROJECT

Not Just a Toy.
A Living Companion.

RoboPet AI is an AI-driven robotic pet designed to provide emotional support, interactive storytelling, and companionship for children and the elderly.

Abstract

RoboPet AI is an innovative AI-driven robotic pet designed to bridge the emotional gap in our increasingly digital world. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and smart sensors, RoboPet AI interacts with users, detects their emotional state, and engages them in interactive storytelling.

Unlike traditional toys and basic robotic devices, our robot provides meaningful companionship through lifelike warmth, adaptive storytelling, and emotion-aware touch responses. It serves as a smart, empathetic companion for children and the elderly, promoting mental well-being.

This project focuses on the seamless integration of hardware and software to create a compact, affordable, and highly responsive robotic companion that feels alive and attentive to its owner.

OUR UNIQUENESS

What Makes RoboPet AI Unique?

Five features that separate us from every other robotic pet in the market.

❤️

Lifelike Warmth & Heartbeat

Equipped with a heating element and vibration motor, our robot provides actual warmth (37°C) and heartbeat-like vibrations when hugged, creating a true emotional connection.

📖

Adaptive Storytelling

Powered by Large Language Models (LLMs), our robot generates stories in real-time. If a user changes the plot, the robot adapts instantly — unlike scripted toys.

🖐️

Emotion-Aware Touch

Self-capacitive tactile sensors allow the robot to distinguish between gentle petting and rough tapping. It reacts with happiness or fear, mimicking a real pet.

🔧

Modular & Low-Cost

Our robot's modular design means a broken sensor can be replaced individually. Target budget: ৮,০০০-১০,০০০ BDT — affordable for everyone.

💚

Mental Well-being Focus

Research shows robotic pets reduce anxiety and heart rate. RoboPet AI is designed as a therapeutic companion for children and the elderly, not just a toy.

🚀

The Future of Companionship

RoboPet AI is not just a project — it is a vision for a smarter, more empathetic future where technology truly understands human emotions.
